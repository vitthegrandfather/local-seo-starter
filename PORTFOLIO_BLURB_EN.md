# LocalSEO Starter — a local plumbing website with technical SEO

I built AquaFix Pro as a portfolio demo for a fictional plumbing business in Kraków, Poland.
The project includes seven Polish pages, with three service landing pages covering scope, process, sample prices, and FAQs.
I implemented unique metadata, canonical URLs, Open Graph, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, and Plumber, Service, BreadcrumbList, and FAQPage JSON-LD.
The responsive interface includes mobile click-to-call and a contact form with client and server validation that defaults to an honest demo mode.
Its map reproduces 18 Kraków districts from municipal geodata, with the source and dataset date shown beside it.
Built with Next.js and TypeScript, the project is ready for Vercel; its production build, 11 browser checks, and 12 logic tests passed.
The business and offer are fictional, and this case makes no claim of real rankings, traffic, leads, or completed client work.
